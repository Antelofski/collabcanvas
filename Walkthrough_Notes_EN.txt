COLLABCANVAS — PLAN, TEST, DEPLOY AND ADAPT

169.5 seconds. English product screens and captions; no voice-over.
Actual prototype states captured through the Chinese Next-only tutorial. Each Planner input is shown separately. Rule selection is separate from data collection. Before/after snapshots preserve identical source work and are read-only; only Apply changes the classroom. The role-play learning activity is context, not the focus.

Testing runs real scripted browser journeys against isolated test state. Guided playback adds a short pause on interface loads so execution is visible; checks are unchanged. It is not an autonomous LLM agent or a teaching-quality assessment. Generation, semantic editing, rule suggestions and interface adaptations use prepared local logic. Students and responses are labelled examples; follow-up responses are simulated, not evidence of learning gains.

The instructor sees contribution, linked-question and conditional reasoning checks. Missing work and agreement are review signals, not diagnoses. The teacher previews changes, applies to the relevant learner or group, and inspects subsequent work. Existing work and private initial answers are preserved. The final knowledge-state report retains individual task evidence, item-level results, teacher judgments and suggested next checks. It does not infer mastery from group completion. Saved snapshots are immutable and can be downloaded. The video uses captured UI geometry for cues, shown immediately with the relevant frame. No controls or responses are fabricated in postproduction.
